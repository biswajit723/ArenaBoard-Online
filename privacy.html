Privacy Policy

JIT ESPORTS · Last updated: 21 November 2026

JIT ESPORTS is a free tournament app for custom-room matches. There are no entry fees and no payments. This page explains what we store and why.

What we collect

  • Your in-game name and game UID, so the admin can check you in the custom room.
  • A login PIN, stored only in encrypted (hashed) form. Nobody, including the admin, can read it.
  • Match data: matches you join, check-ins, results, kills, points, reports and chat messages inside a match.
  • If you turn on notifications, a browser push address so we can send match alerts.

How we use it

  • To run matches, show leaderboards and send match notifications.
  • To keep matches fair (reports, warnings and bans).
  • The admin posts match banners and public results (in-game names, points and kills) on JIT ESPORTS social channels such as Telegram, Discord, Facebook and Instagram.

What we do not do

  • We do not sell your data or share it with advertisers.
  • We do not ask for your phone number, email, game password or payment details.
  • Our Facebook/Instagram connection is used only to publish posts on our own Page and account. We do not read or collect data from other users through it.

Where data is stored

Data is stored securely with our database provider (Supabase). The website is hosted on Vercel.

Deleting your data

To delete your account and data, message the admin in the JIT ESPORTS WhatsApp group or Telegram channel with your game UID. We will delete it within 7 days. Past public results posts may stay on social media.

Contact

Questions? Contact the JIT ESPORTS admin through the WhatsApp group or the contact link on the app's Home screen.
